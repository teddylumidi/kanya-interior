How we work

From first message to handover

  1. Talk to us

    Send photos and a short description on WhatsApp, or book a site visit so we can see the space and take measurements.

  2. Design & quote

    We agree the design, materials and finishes with you and give you a clear quotation before any work starts.

  3. Build & install

    Our team builds and installs everything on site, working to the agreed design and finishes.

  4. Finish & handover

    We complete the final details, clean up and walk you through the finished space.
